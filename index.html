A neighborhood gourmet place, done right.

Armonk Country Kitchen is your go-to spot for breakfast, lunch, take-home dinners, and everything in between. Start your morning with our griddle favorites, build your own creation at our all-day salad bar, or choose from a sandwich menu deep enough to satisfy the whole neighborhood. Everything is freshly made in-house daily-from our scratch-baked muffins and breads to our famous, comforting hot soups (available seasonal from September through May)
